Evulto — Marketing Copy & Campaign Kit

Single source of truth for how we describe Evulto. Adapt tone per channel; keep the substance identical.

1. The Hook (1 line)

An AI fitness coach that actually calls your phone — and corrects your form in real time.

Use as: video opener, ad headline, Reddit/PH title, the first sentence of any DM.

Shorter variants

  • "Your AI coach literally calls you. And watches your form."
  • "The fitness app that calls you like a real coach."

2. The Pitch (3 lines)

Most fitness apps are silent checklists you ignore.
Evulto gives you an AI coach with a personality who calls your phone to get you training, corrects your form live through your camera, and turns your runs into a map you conquer.
One-time $1.99 for a founding Vanguard pass — first 500 only, then it's gone.

5. What NOT to say

  • No "lifetime free / lifetime premium" — promise is one-time $1.99 + 1 month free Legend at launch, then Free or $19.99/mo.
  • Don't name real fitness celebrities. Coaches are original personas (Sculptor, Monument, Analyst, Commander, Architect).
  • No medical/clinical outcome claims.
