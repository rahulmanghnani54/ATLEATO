## Variables to Replace

Use your email platform's merge tags:

- `{POSITION}` — waitlist position number
- `{EMAIL}` — recipient email
- `{FIRST_NAME}` — first name
- `{FRIEND_COUNT}` — referrals count
- `{TOTAL_SIGNUPS}` — total waitlist count
- `{VIP_CLAIMED}` — Vanguard Passes claimed (start at 412)
- `{VIP_REMAINING}` — 500 minus VIP_CLAIMED
- `{WORKOUT_COUNT}` — user's workout count from app
