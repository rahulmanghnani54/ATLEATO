## Long Bio (300 words — for keynote / podcast / interview)

```
Rahul Manghnani is the founder and solo developer of Atleato (atleato.com), an AI-powered fitness coaching app launched on Google Play in 2026.

Rahul spent 12 months building Atleato single-handedly — writing every line of code, designing every screen, and producing every piece of marketing content. The project was bootstrapped with $0 in external funding, with total spend limited to a $25 Google Play developer fee and a $15 domain registration.

The product was born from a personal frustration: for three years, Rahul slept through his 5 AM gym alarm every morning. After realizing that nobody dismisses a phone CALL the way they dismiss a notification, he built an AI coaching system that uses Android's ConnectionService API to escalate wake-up reminders into actual incoming-call experiences — full screen, full ringtone, persistent vibration, with automatic 5-minute callback if declined. Beta testing with 47 users showed a wake-up rate improvement from 12% to 91%.

Atleato distinguishes itself through five distinct AI coach personas — each with their own color scheme, training philosophy, nutrition style, signature lifts, and voice tone. Switching coaches dynamically retunes the entire application, effectively shipping five different fitness experiences in one binary. The app also features on-device computer vision form correction (using Google's MoveNet pose detection model), territory-based GPS gameplay inspired by Splatoon, and an RPG-style progression system.

Beyond Atleato, Rahul is an active "build in public" advocate who shares behind-the-scenes development on Twitter/X (@rahulmanghnani). He believes the future of fitness software is persona-driven, on-device, and refreshingly opinionated — rejecting the "neutral fitness coach" model that has dominated the category for the past decade.

He is based in India and is currently the only employee of Atleato.
```

## Headshot Brief

You need ONE professional headshot. Here's the spec:

### What it should look like
- **Background:** Dark gray or black (matches Atleato's brand)
- **Lighting:** Dramatic side lighting (Rembrandt or split lighting), NOT flat
- **Expression:** Neutral confidence, slight smile or serious. NO cheesy grin.
- **Wardrobe:** Plain dark t-shirt (black, dark gray, or charcoal). NO logos, NO collared shirts.
- **Framing:** Head + shoulders. Centered. Lots of headroom.
- **Resolution:** 2000×2000 minimum (allows crop to square for socials)

### Options to get it

**Option 1: HeyGen AI Photo (free trial — $24/mo)**
1. Go to heygen.com/photo-avatar
2. Upload 5-10 selfies of yourself (different angles, lighting)
3. Use this prompt:
```
Professional founder headshot, dark gray background, dramatic side lighting (Rembrandt style), wearing a plain black t-shirt, neutral confident expression, head and shoulders framing, shot on Hasselblad 80mm lens, hyperrealistic, magazine quality, sharp focus
```

**Option 2: Local photographer (~₹2,000-5,000 in India)**
- Search Google for "corporate headshot photographer near me"
- Book 30-min session
- Bring 3 t-shirts (black, dark gray, charcoal)
- Ask for: "Founder-style headshot, dramatic lighting, dark background"

**Option 3: Self-shot with iPhone Portrait Mode (free)**
- Find a wall with bright window light to your right
- iPhone on Portrait mode, lens at chest height
- 50-100 shots, varying expressions
- Edit in Lightroom Mobile (free): boost contrast, reduce highlights, add slight orange tone
- This is genuinely workable if you're tight on budget
