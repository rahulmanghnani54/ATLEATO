About

Evulto is the AI coach that actually calls you.

An independent AI fitness app & software brand · Android

Evulto is an independent AI fitness app and software brand. It puts five world-class AI coach personas in your pocket — and unlike every other app, they don't send a notification you swipe away. They phone-call you: a real, full-screen, ringing call to get you up for a workout, correct your form in real time through your camera, scan your meals for macros, and back off on your rest days.

What the name means

"Evulto" is our own coined brand name — it carries the spirit of exult: the rush of triumph when you push through a session and rise to your best. It's an independent AI fitness app and software brand for Android, built on one idea — show up, and feel that win every day.

Why we built it

Most fitness apps track workouts. We wanted to build something that actually motivates people to show up. Evulto combines AI coaching, real-time form feedback, personalized motivation, and gamified challenges into one experience designed to help athletes stay consistent — the accountability of a real coach, in software.

Our mission is simple: help people train harder, move better, and stay accountable — every single day.

What makes Evulto different

  • It calls you. Real wake-up and workout calls that ring like an actual phone call — with a callback if you decline.
  • Five distinct coaches. Switch personas and the whole app transforms — voice, programming, nutrition philosophy, and motivation style.
  • Real-time form correction. On-device, 33-joint pose tracking checks your form rep by rep — and your camera frames never leave your phone.
  • Rest-day aware. It reads your plan and stays silent when you should be recovering. A coach, not a nag.
