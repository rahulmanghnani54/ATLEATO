What is Evulto? +

An AI fitness coaching app for Android. Five coach personas phone-call you awake and for workouts, correct your form in real time using your camera, scan your meals for macros, and only call on training days.

How do the wake-up calls work? +

Set a time, pick a ringtone. Your phone rings like an actual incoming call — full screen and vibration. Decline and your coach calls back in five minutes. It's not a notification you can swipe away.

Is it really free? +

Yes — the Free tier includes one coach, workouts, nutrition tracking, and wake-up & workout calls, forever. No credit card required.

It's our coined brand name, evoking exult — the rush of triumph when you push through and rise to your best.
