## Hashtag Strategy

Use 8-15 hashtags per post. Mix:
- **Big** (1-3): #fitness, #gymmotivation, #workout (10M+ posts)
- **Medium** (5-7): #fitnesstech, #aifitness, #fitnessapp, #fitnessjourney (100K-1M posts)
- **Niche** (3-5): #buildinpublic, #fitnesscoach, #classicphysique, #posedetection (10K-100K posts)
- **Branded** (1): #atleato (yours)

Don't put hashtags in caption — put in first comment for cleaner look.
