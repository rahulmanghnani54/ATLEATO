## Short Description Optimization (80 chars)

Pack 3-4 Tier 1 keywords into the short description. Current:

> "5 AI legend coaches. Live form check. Real wake-up calls. No excuses."
> *(67 chars, 3 keywords: "AI", "coaches", "wake-up calls")*

Better options:

```
"AI fitness coach app with live form check & wake-up calls. 5 coaches. Free."
(78 chars, 5 keywords)

"AI workout coach app: live form check, wake-up calls, 5 personas. Free."
(72 chars, 5 keywords)

"AI fitness coach + form correction + gym tracker. 5 coach personas. Free."
(75 chars, 6 keywords)
```

**Recommended:** Option 3. Highest keyword density without sounding spammy.

## Banned Words / Don't Use

Google Play's algorithm flags these — using them gets you de-ranked or rejected:

❌ "Best fitness app"
❌ "#1 fitness app"
❌ "Top rated"
❌ "Award winning"
❌ "Free download" (use just "Free")
❌ "Better than [competitor]"
❌ Any actual celebrity name (THE SCULPTOR, The Governor, etc) — IP + ASO penalty
❌ "Cure", "treat", "diagnose" — Health & Fitness category guideline
