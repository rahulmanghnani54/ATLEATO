# ProductHunt Launch — Atleato

## Pre-Launch Setup (do 2-3 weeks before)

1. Create ProductHunt account 60+ days before launch (anti-spam)
2. Get 20-30 friends/connections to follow you on PH
3. Submit Atleato to Coming Soon page 2 weeks ahead
4. DM 10 power users (people with 1000+ upvotes given) week-of for support
5. Build a small Telegram/Discord group of "launch hunters" — ask them to be online at launch time

## Launch Day = Tuesday or Wednesday (12:01 AM PST)

ProductHunt resets at midnight Pacific Time. Submit AT 12:01 AM PST for full 24-hour visibility.

## Goal Metrics

- **#1 of the day** is unrealistic without a marketing budget. Don't chase it.
- **Top 5** = great. ~300-500 upvotes typically.
- **Top 10** = good. ~150 upvotes.
- **Featured** = anywhere on the front page = win.

## After the Launch

- Day +1: thank-you post on PH "thanks for #X" — drives more visibility
- Day +7: send a "what we shipped this week" update to all your PH followers
- Day +30: "What 30 days on Product Hunt taught me" — Medium/LinkedIn post

## Common Mistakes

❌ Vote brigade your own product (instant ban)
❌ Pay for upvotes (PH catches this 100% of the time)
❌ Beg friends for upvotes in DMs day-of (they forget — ask 24 hours BEFORE)
❌ Disappear from comments — stay engaged FOR 24 STRAIGHT HOURS
❌ Launch on Monday (low traffic) or Friday (people log off)
❌ Forget to include a Lemon Squeezy / Stripe link in the comment for instant conversion
