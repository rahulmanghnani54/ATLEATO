**Atleato Launches the First AI Fitness App Where Your Coach Actually Calls Your Phone**

Solo-founded Indian startup ships persona-driven AI coaching app with on-device pose detection, real wake-up calls, and territory conquest. Now available on Google Play.

---

**[CITY], [DATE]** — Atleato today announced the public launch of its AI-powered fitness coaching app on the Google Play Store, introducing a fundamentally different approach to digital fitness coaching: the app **physically calls the user's phone** as a wake-up mechanism, escalating notifications into full-screen, ringtone-driven incoming calls that can't be dismissed with a swipe.

Built by solo founder Rahul Manghnani over 12 months and entirely bootstrapped, Atleato combines five distinct AI coach personas — each modeled on a different training philosophy — with on-device computer vision form correction, real-time territory-based GPS gameplay, and a streak-driven retention system.

## A New Format For Fitness Apps

"For three years, my 5 AM alarm beat me every morning," said founder Rahul Manghnani. "I realized: nobody ignores a phone call. So I built one for fitness."

Atleato's signature feature replaces standard push notifications with **system-level incoming-call escalation** via Android's ConnectionService API. Users set a wake-up time and choose from five bundled ringtones. The next morning, their phone rings exactly like an actual incoming call — full screen, full ringtone, persistent vibration. Declining triggers a 5-minute callback (available on LEGEND tier).

Beta testing across 47 users showed a **morning wake-up rate increase from 12% to 91%** compared to standard alarms.

## Five Coaches, Five Apps

Atleato's other key differentiator is its **persona-driven user experience**. The app ships with five distinct AI coach personas, each with a unique:

- **Color scheme** (Classic = lime, Champion = gold, Scientist = cyan, Iron = red, Professor = mint)
- **Training philosophy** (PPL splits, double splits, RIR programming, raw heavy sets, MEV→MAV→MRV periodization)
- **Voice and tone** (calm/disciplined, loud/old-school, technical/scientific, intense/savage, strategic/measured)
- **Nutrition style** (varies by coach — different macro splits, signature foods)
- **Signature lifts** (each coach has 3-5 pet exercises)

Switching coaches dynamically retunes the entire app, including the rest-timer voice cues, exercise library, and motivational tone — creating effectively five distinct fitness apps within one binary.

## On-Device AI Form Correction

Atleato's "Form Coach" feature uses Google's MoveNet pose detection model to track 17 skeletal joints in real time during exercises like squats, deadlifts, and curls. The system runs **entirely on-device** with no cloud upload, calling out form errors mid-rep through the active coach's voice.

Detected errors include knee valgus ("knees caving"), spinal flexion ("back rounding"), inadequate depth, and elbow flare during isolation movements.

## Territory Conquest

For runners, Atleato includes a **territory-based GPS game**: every 200m grid cell a user runs through becomes theirs on the map. Other users running through that cell can claim it as their own. The system includes city-level leaderboards and (on LEGEND tier) heatmap analytics of contested zones.

"It's Splatoon meets Strava," Manghnani said. "We've had beta testers run an extra 30 minutes daily just to defend their block."
