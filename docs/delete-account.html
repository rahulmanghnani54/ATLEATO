DATA & ACCOUNT

Delete your account

You can permanently delete your Evulto account and all associated data at any time. Here's how.

Developer: Evulto (Rahul Manghnani). App: Evulto — AI Fitness Coach (com.madsales.atleato).

Delete from inside the app (instant)

  1. Open Evulto and go to Profile.
  2. Scroll to the Account section.
  3. Tap Delete account, then confirm Delete forever.

Your account is erased immediately and irreversibly, and you're signed out.

Don't have the app installed?

Email hello@evulto.com from the address on your account with the subject "Delete my account". We verify the request and delete everything within 7 days.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes all of the following:

  • Your account & profile (email, name, body stats, goals)
  • Workout logs, sets, personal records, and progression history
  • Nutrition & water logs
  • Recovery check-ins and streaks
  • Physique check-ins and analyses (your encrypted photos)
  • Achievements, territory data, and push tokens

This happens via a cascading database delete, so no orphaned data remains. Nothing is retained after deletion except where we're legally required to keep limited records (e.g. payment receipts held by our payment processor). See our Privacy Policy for details.
