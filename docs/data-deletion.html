DATA & ACCOUNT DELETION

Delete your account and data.

You're always in control of your data. Here's exactly how to permanently delete your Evulto account and everything we hold — whether you signed up with email, Google, or Facebook.

LAST UPDATED · 2 JULY 2026

TL;DR: Open the app → Profile → Delete Account to erase everything instantly. No app access? Email hello@evulto.com from your registered address and we'll delete it within 7 days.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (instant)

  1. Open the Evulto app.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

This immediately and permanently deletes your account and all associated data from our systems. It cannot be undone.

Option 2 — Delete by email

Email hello@evulto.com from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my data". We'll verify the request, delete your account and all associated data, and confirm by reply within 7 days.

If you signed in with Google or Facebook

Deleting your account (Option 1 or 2 above) removes all data Evulto holds about you, including the link to your Google or Facebook login. You can additionally revoke Evulto's access from your provider's settings:

  • Google: myaccount.google.com → Data & privacy → Third-party apps & services → Evulto → Remove access.
  • Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites → Evulto → Remove.

Revoking access alone stops future sign-ins but does not delete data — use Option 1 or 2 for that.

What gets deleted

  • Your login / authentication account (email, Google, or Facebook link)
  • Profile & body stats (name, height, weight, goals)
  • Workout logs, nutrition logs, and recovery check-ins
  • Physique check-in photos (already encrypted on your device)
  • Streaks, achievements, referral, and squad data
  • Push notification tokens and scheduled coach calls

What we may retain

We keep only what the law requires (for example, payment/transaction records for tax and accounting), and only for as long as we're legally required to. Any retained records are the minimum necessary and are not used to profile you.
