Cookie Policy

Last updated June 29, 2026

This page explains the cookies and similar local-storage technologies the Evulto website (atleato.com) uses, and how you control them. For how we handle personal data overall, see our Privacy Policy.

What are cookies & local storage?

Cookies are small files a site stores in your browser. "Local storage" is a related browser feature we use for the same purposes. Both let the site remember small pieces of information between page loads.

Essential — always on

These are required for the site to work and do not track you across other sites. They run without consent because the site can't function correctly without them.

  • Cookie consent preference (atleato_cookie_consent) — remembers whether you accepted analytics, so we don't ask again on every visit.
  • Anti-spam rate limit — a short-lived counter that stops the waitlist form from being submitted abusively.

Optional — analytics (only after you accept)

If you click "Accept All" in our cookie banner, we may enable privacy-respecting analytics to understand what's working and improve the site. If you choose "Essential Only," none of these load and no analytics data is sent. When enabled, they may include:

  • Google Analytics 4 — anonymous, aggregated page views, scroll depth, and conversion events (with IP anonymization).
  • Meta Pixel — measures the effectiveness of our Instagram/Facebook ads.
  • Microsoft Clarity — anonymized heatmaps and aggregate behavior to find usability problems.

We never put your email or other personal details into analytics events.

How to control cookies

  • On this site: choose "Essential Only" in the cookie banner to decline analytics. To change your mind later, clear this site's data in your browser and the banner will reappear.
  • In your browser: you can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time.
  • Google Analytics opt-out: install Google's opt-out browser add-on.
