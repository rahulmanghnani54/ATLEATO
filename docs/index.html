FAQ

Questions?
Answered.

What is Evulto? +

Evulto is an AI fitness coaching app for Android. Five world-class coach personas phone-call you awake and for workouts, correct your exercise form in real time using your camera, scan your meals for macros, and adapt to your training plan so they never disturb you on rest days.

What does the name "Evulto" mean? +

Evulto is our own coined brand name — it carries the spirit of "exult": the rush of triumph when you push through a workout and rise to your best. It's an independent AI fitness app for Android with five AI coach personas that call you, correct your form, and keep you showing up.

How does Evulto's AI form correction work? +

Evulto uses your phone's camera and on-device pose detection to track your joints during a set. It analyzes your movement live and flags issues — like knees caving in or a shallow squat — so you fix your form rep by rep, without a human trainer in the room.

What makes Evulto different from other AI fitness apps? +

Most fitness apps send notifications you swipe away. Evulto actually rings your phone like a real call — full-screen, ringtone, and a callback if you decline. It pairs that with five switchable coach personas, real-time form correction, and rest-day-aware scheduling in one app.

Is Evulto really free? +

Yes. The Free tier includes 1 coach, workouts, nutrition tracking, wake-up and workout calls, and streaks — forever. No credit card required. PRO and LEGEND unlock additional coaches, AI form correction, and premium features.

How do Evulto's wake-up calls work? +

Set a time, pick a ringtone. Your phone rings like an actual incoming call — full screen, vibration, the works. If you decline, your coach calls back 5 minutes later. It's not a notification you can swipe away.

Will Evulto bug me on rest days? +

No. Evulto reads your training plan and only calls on workout days — your rest days stay completely silent. It's a coach, not a nag: it pushes when it counts and lets you recover when it matters.

Can I switch coaches in Evulto? +

Free users get 1 coach. PRO unlocks 3. LEGEND gets all 5. When you switch, the entire app transforms — colors, workout programs, nutrition philosophy, voice, and motivational style all change to match your new coach.

When does Evulto launch? +

Evulto is in closed beta. Join the waitlist to get early access. Waitlist members will be the first to download when it launches on Google Play.
